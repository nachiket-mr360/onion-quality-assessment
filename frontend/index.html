    About ONIONVISION

    ONIONVISION · SIH26031 · Team FANTASTIC6

    Technical notes

    • RGB visible-surface assessment only.
    • RGB cannot directly observe hidden/internal onion quality.
    • Physical diameter requires a calibration reference (ArUco preferred).
    • Size does not change GOOD/BAD.
    • Low-confidence results can be marked REVIEW_REQUIRED (a review state, not a third grade).
    • Locked classes: HEALTHY, DAMAGED, ROTTEN, SPROUTED.

    Camera · OpenCV · YOLO · FastAPI · SQLite · HTML/CSS/JS
