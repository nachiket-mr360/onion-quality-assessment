    From visible quality to deeper quality intelligence

    Current prototype — RGB

    • Visible damage, rot, sprouting, healthy appearance
    • Visual classification and batch statistics
    • Does not observe hidden / internal quality

    Planned — NIR / multispectral

    Planned extension for additional spectral information that could support assessment of hidden/internal quality indicators. Not implemented or validated in this prototype.

    Future vision

    RGB + NIR/multispectral + size + quality rules + digital records → multimodal onion quality intelligence.

    Technical notes

    • RGB visible-surface assessment only.
    • RGB cannot directly observe hidden/internal onion quality.
    • Physical diameter requires a calibration reference (ArUco preferred).
    • Size does not change GOOD/BAD.
    • Low-confidence results can be marked REVIEW_REQUIRED (a review state, not a third grade).
    • Locked classes: HEALTHY, DAMAGED, ROTTEN, SPROUTED.
